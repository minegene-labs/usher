# Getting help

## Ask a question

Open an issue on this repository's Issues tab and choose the Question form. It asks what you want to do and what happens instead, and gives the issue the `question` label. If something is plainly broken, use the bug template instead.

## What to include

- The version: Settings → About shows it.
- What you were trying to do and what you already tried.
- The relevant lines from Status → Logs, with anything secret removed.

## What not to post

Passwords, API keys, tokens, session cookies, push keys, and your server's address or anything else that identifies you or your home. If a log line has one, black it out before pasting. Never put a security problem in a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Look here first

- Setting up or changing `usher.toml`: [docs/configuration.md](docs/configuration.md).
- Installing, updating and upgrading: [deploy/README.md](deploy/README.md).
- A security problem, such as a way to get into someone else's account: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
